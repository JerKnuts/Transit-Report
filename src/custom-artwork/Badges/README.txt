TRANSIT REPORT - CUSTOM BADGES

Put PNG, JPG, JPEG or animated GIF images with any filenames in:
Documents/My Games/Starfield/Transit Report/Artwork/Badges/

Choose Custom for this slot in OSF Settings, Gameplay Options or the installer. Run Starfield once to create the folders, and fully restart after adding, removing or replacing images. Up to 64 files per folder; one valid image is randomly chosen per loading screen. Immediate repeats are avoided when alternatives exist during the current session.

Transparent PNG is suitable for badges/indicators. Still badges stay still; still indicators rotate. Animated GIFs play their own looping animation. Backgrounds fill the screen without stretching and receive a dark lower gradient.

No external importer, Python or manual SWF conversion is needed. Generated files belong in the game's Data/Interface/TransitReport/Cache folder (MO2 may redirect them to Overwrite); source images belong in Documents. Empty/invalid pools fall back to Orbiting Star or Orbital Chart.

Still-image limits: 64 MB, 16 megapixels, with output reduced to at most 2560px. GIF limits: 240 frames, 60 seconds, 2048px canvas and 16 megapixels across all decoded frames; runtime animation at most 512px with 30fps timing. APNG animation is not supported.
